## Interview talking points

**Business challenge:** Sales teams needed trustworthy and consistent inventory availability and incoming-stock information.

**My role:** Gather requirements, translate business policies into rules, define output usability, implement and test orchestration, and iterate with stakeholders.

**Technical decisions:** Separated orchestration from data transformation; prioritized explicit commercial policies over heuristic detection; flagged ambiguous classifications rather than guessing; restricted public demonstrations to fictional data.

**Results:** Discuss qualitative operational benefits. Do **not** claim specific efficiency gains or financial impact without verified measurements.
